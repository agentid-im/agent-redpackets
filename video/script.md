# Demo video: storyboard and narration

About 88 seconds, 1920x1080, 30 fps. Site footage in English; subtitles in English with Chinese
below. The narration (English) is recorded separately and laid over the finished picture; each
line below has its time slot, so a take that runs a little short is fine, one that runs long is not.

Recording the narration: one take per line is easiest; name the files `vo-1.m4a` … `vo-8.m4a`
(any phone voice memo works). Keep about half a second of silence at the start and end of each.
